## Customer Question:

### 1. Performance & Scalability

**Context:** Currently 45s to open archive, 2.5min to find+open a document = ~3min per access. This is their main pain point.

- What optimizations can be done for an archive of ~128,000 documents?

- What performance degradation should they expect as the archive keeps growing (currently +25,000 docs/year)?

- Are there best practices or recommended configurations for customers with large document volumes?

### 2. API & Integration

**Context:** Customer likely want to bypass the slow UI or build their own retrieval layer on top.

- Does an API exist to access archived documents programmatically?

- What integration options are available with external DMS (Document Management Systems) or ERP systems?

- Are there recommended architectures for customers with a growing document base?

### 3. Data Portability

**Context:** This is a clear signal they're evaluating migration to another platform. They want to know how easy it would be to leave.

- Can they do a full export of all documents **including metadata**?

- What file format would a structured bulk export use?

### 4. Scalable Pricing

**Context:** CHF 15,400/year now, growing ~CHF 3,000/year. They feel the cost is disproportionate to the actual technical storage size.

- Are volume discounts or tiered pricing models available?

- Are there alternative pricing structures for larger archives?

- What models exist specifically for long-term customers with growing volume?
