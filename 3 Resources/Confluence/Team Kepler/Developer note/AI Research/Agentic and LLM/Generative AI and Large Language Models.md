# Generative AI and Large Language Models

*Confluence source · Team Kepler › Developer note › AI Research › Agentic and LLM · [view original](https://axonivy.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/TK/pages/48858366032/Generative+AI+and+Large+Language+Models) · updated 2025-11-10*

#### **I. Foundational Technology**

- **Transformer Architecture (2017)**

- Self-attention mechanism enables processing of longer sequences

- Encoder-decoder structure for text transformation

- Overcame memory limitations of previous models

- **Scale Evolution**

- BERT: 110 million parameters

- GPT-4: Hundreds of billions of parameters

- PaLM 2: Up to 340 billion parameters

#### **II. Core Capabilities**

- **Content Generation**

- Text, code, images, audio, video

- Novel outputs based on learned patterns

- Human-like creative abilities

- **In-Context Learning**

- Adapts to new tasks from few examples

- No parameter updates required

- Contains "hidden" trainable models within architecture

- Larger models override semantic priors better than smaller ones

#### **III. Economic Impact**

- **Market Potential**

- \$2.6-4.4 trillion annual value by 2030

- 70% of business activities potentially automated

- **Affected Sectors**

- Knowledge work: lawyers, analysts, educators

- Creative fields: content creation, design

- Technical roles: software development, data analysis

#### **IV. Implementation Techniques**

- **Prompt Engineering**

- Zero-shot: Direct instructions only

- Few-shot: Learning from examples

- Chain-of-thought: Step-by-step reasoning

- Iterative refinement critical for optimization

- **Enterprise Deployment**

- Google Vertex AI and Model Garden

- LangChain integration for complex reasoning

- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA)

#### **V. Critical Challenges**

- **Technical Limitations**

- Hallucination: Plausible but false information

- Lack of explainability in decision-making

- Struggles with mathematical precision

- **Implementation Barriers**

- 76% companies experimenting (2023)

- Only 9% achieved production deployment

- Integration complexity with existing systems

#### **VI. Research Breakthroughs**

- **Stanford-Google Agents Study**

- 25 AI agents simulating human behavior

- Autonomous social interactions

- Memory, reflection, and planning capabilities

- Emergent complex behaviors (organizing parties, forming relationships)

#### **VII. Future Direction**

**Specialization Trend**

- Moving from general-purpose to domain-specific

- Industry-tailored solutions (e.g., GitHub Copilot for developers)

- Better performance through focused applications

- **Governance Evolution**

- EU AI Act requirements

- US executive orders on AI safety

- Growing emphasis on responsible deployment

#### **VIII. Success Factors**

- **Essential Requirements**

- Robust data governance

- Human oversight for critical applications

- Clear use case identification

- Risk management frameworks

- **Best Practices**

- Start with pilot projects

- Focus on specific, measurable outcomes

- Maintain realistic expectations

- Prioritize user trust and safety
